IPTV Smarters Pro Setup Guide for Pakistani Users

August 2026 · 5 min read

Step 1: Download IPTV Smarters Pro

It's free and available on the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, Amazon Fire TV app store, and most Smart TV app stores (Samsung, LG).

Step 2: Choose "Login with Xtream Codes API"

When you first open the app, you'll see a few login options. Choose Xtream Codes API (the most common method) and enter the server URL, username and password we send you after payment.

Step 3: Wait for Channels to Load

The app will sync your channel list, movies and series — this usually takes under a minute on a good connection.

Step 4: Organize Your Favourites

Long-press any channel to add it to Favourites — this makes switching between GEO, ARY, HUM and PTV Sports much faster during matches or drama time.

Troubleshooting

If channels don't load, double check your login details for typos, confirm your subscription hasn't expired, and make sure your internet connection is stable. Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp — we troubleshoot setup issues directly.
