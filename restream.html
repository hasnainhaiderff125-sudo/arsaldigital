The basics

What Is Restream?

Restream means receiving an authorized live stream and passing it on to one or more destinations through suitable infrastructure. Instead of every viewer or platform connecting to the original source on its own, the stream is received once, managed centrally and delivered where it needs to go, such as different regions, platforms or business locations.

It sounds technical, but the idea is simple: one source in, organized distribution out. Compare that with regular IPTV services, where the goal is for a person to watch channels on their own device.

Restreaming is only appropriate for content you have the right to distribute. See the rights question in the FAQ below.

International Channel Restream

Different markets want different content. A household in Lahore, a hotel in Dubai and a streaming business serving viewers in Germany will each need a different mix of channels, languages and delivery settings. A good Restream setup starts from those differences instead of forcing one lineup everywhere.

Regional content

Content that matters mainly to one country or community, such as local news or regional entertainment.

International channels

Channels produced in one country and watched in another, common with diaspora audiences and multilingual households.

Language-based content

Grouping by language, such as Urdu, Arabic, English or German, so viewers find what they understand.

Country-specific content

Content tied to the licensing terms of a particular country, which is why availability differs from market to market.

Multi-region distribution

Serving several markets from one organized setup instead of running a separate one for each.

Channel organization

Categories, regions and ordering that keep large lineups easy to browse and manage.

FAQ

Restream Questions, Answered

What is Restream?
Restream is the process of receiving an authorized stream and distributing it to one or more destinations or regions through suitable infrastructure. It lets you organize and deliver streams from one central setup instead of connecting every destination to the original source separately.
How does Restream work?
A source stream is connected, managed in a central setup and then delivered to the intended destination or region. The exact steps depend on the infrastructure and package you choose, so tell us your requirements and we will explain what is involved.
What is an IPTV Restream service?
An IPTV Restream service applies the Restream model to IP-delivered TV streams: authorized channel feeds are received, organized and passed on to the destinations you specify. It suits businesses that distribute streams. If you simply want to watch TV, our IPTV packages are the better starting point.
Can I access channels from different countries?
International channels can be part of a Restream setup, depending on your package, the provider and the rights that apply. Availability differs by country and channel, so tell us the regions you need and we will confirm what is currently possible.
Which countries are supported?
This page covers requirements in South Asia, the Middle East, Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific and Africa, including Pakistan, Germany, Egypt, the UK and the USA. Support for a specific country, channel category or destination is confirmed case by case, so ask before you plan around it.
Can I request specific channel categories?
Yes. Tell us which categories you are interested in, such as news, sports, entertainment, movies, documentary or kids and family, and we will tell you what is available for your region and package. We do not guarantee any particular channel.
What is the difference between IPTV and Restream?
Traditional IPTV generally focuses on consuming streams: a viewer watches channels on their own device. Restream solutions focus on receiving, managing and redistributing authorized streams to specific destinations. Both are legitimate models that serve different needs.
Can Restream be used for business distribution?
Restream is built around distribution, so it can suit streaming businesses, content distributors, media platforms, hospitality venues and authorized resellers. Whether your specific use is permitted depends on the rights, licences and provider terms that apply to you.
Are all channels available in every country?
No. Channel and regional availability may vary based on package, provider, licensing, rights and current service availability. A channel offered in one country should not be assumed to be available in another.
Do I need content rights for redistribution?
Yes. Redistributing a stream generally requires that you hold, or are covered by, the rights or licence to do so. You are responsible for making sure your use complies with applicable law and provider terms. If you are unsure, take legal advice before you start.
