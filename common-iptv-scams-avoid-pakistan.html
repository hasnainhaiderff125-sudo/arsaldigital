Common IPTV Scams in Pakistan — And How to Avoid Them

August 2026 · 5 min read

Red Flag 1: No Free Trial Offered

A legitimate provider is confident enough in their service to offer a short free trial before you pay. If a seller refuses outright, that's a warning sign.

Red Flag 2: Payment Only via Untraceable Methods

Be cautious of sellers who only accept payment methods with no transaction record or refuse to provide any order confirmation.

Red Flag 3: No Real Customer Support

If a seller disappears after payment or takes days to respond, you likely won't get help when something goes wrong later.

Red Flag 4: Prices That Seem Too Good to Be True

Extremely cheap, long-term plans (like a "lifetime" subscription for a few hundred rupees) are almost always unsustainable and tend to stop working within weeks.

What a Trustworthy Provider Looks Like

Clear PKR pricing, a real WhatsApp number with fast responses, a free trial option, and consistent uptime over time are the basic signs of a legitimate service.
