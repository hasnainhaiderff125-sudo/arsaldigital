How to Set Up IPTV on Roku

August 2026 · 4 min read

Why Roku Is Different

Unlike Android-based devices, Roku's app store doesn't allow generic IPTV player apps due to its closed ecosystem. The most reliable option is screen mirroring from your phone or laptop.

Option 1: Screen Mirroring from Android/iPhone

Install IPTV Smarters Pro on your phone, log in with your subscription details, then use Roku's built-in screen mirroring feature (Settings → System → Screen mirroring) to cast your phone screen to the TV.

Option 2: Use a Firestick or Android Box Alongside Roku

If you stream IPTV regularly, plugging a low-cost Firestick or Android TV box into a spare HDMI port on the same TV is often more stable than mirroring.

Which Option Should You Choose?

For occasional viewing, mirroring works fine. For daily use, especially sports and dramas, a dedicated Firestick or Android box gives a smoother, more permanent setup.
