Amazon Firestick & Fire TV

Firestick apps are installed with the Downloader app and a short code.

Confirm your Downloader code on WhatsApp first

Codes change when app developers update their links. We will send you the current working code.

Get my Downloader code

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Allow installs: Settings › My Fire TV › Developer Options › Install unknown apps › turn Downloader ON.
  3. Enter the code we send you in Downloader, press Go, then Install.
  4. Open the app and log in with your details.

No Developer Options? Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and click your device name 7 times.

Samsung & LG Smart TV

Hot Player and IBO Player are the two players we recommend.

Good to know: free IPTV players are not available in the Samsung and LG stores. Hot Player and IBO Player offer a free trial, then a paid activation on their official website. That fee goes to the app developer, not to us.

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Install Hot Player or IBO Player from Samsung Smart Hub or the LG Content Store.
  2. Note the MAC address or Device ID (and Device Key) shown on your TV.
  3. Add the M3U link we send you on the app’s official website, using that ID.
  4. Restart the app on your TV.

Setup FAQs

Which app is best for my device?+
For Android phones, tablets and TVs we suggest XCIPTV Player. On Samsung and LG TVs use IBO Player or Hot Player. For iPhone and iPad, message us and we will guide you.
Why do the Samsung and LG apps ask for payment?+
Free IPTV players are generally not available in the Samsung and LG app stores. The players that work well there, like IBO Player and Hot Player, give a free trial and then ask for a paid activation. That fee goes to the app developer, not to us, so only pay on the app’s official website.
Where do I get the Downloader code for Firestick?+
Downloader codes can change when app developers update their links, so we confirm the current code for you on WhatsApp before you install. Tap any “Get code” button and we will reply with it.
My app says login failed. What should I do?+
Check that the server URL, username and password are typed exactly as we sent them, with no extra spaces. If it still fails, send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we will fix it quickly.
