Confirm your Downloader code on WhatsApp first

App developers change their download links from time to time, so an old code can stop working. Tell us which app you want and we will send you the current, working code.

Get my Downloader code
1

Install Downloader

On your Firestick, open Find, search “Downloader” and install the app from the Amazon Appstore.

2

Allow installs

Go to Settings, My Fire TV, Developer Options, Install unknown apps and turn Downloader ON. If you do not see Developer Options, open Settings, My Fire TV, About and click your device name 7 times.

3

Enter the code

Open Downloader, go to Home, type the code we send you, press Go and then Install.

4

Log in

Samsung & LG Smart TV

Hot Player and IBO Player are the two players we recommend on Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS).

Good to know: free IPTV players are generally not available in the Samsung and LG app stores. Hot Player and IBO Player give you a free trial, then ask for a paid activation. That fee is paid to the app developer on their official website, not to us. Not sure? Ask us before paying.
1

Install the player

Samsung: open Smart Hub, then Apps. LG: open the LG Content Store. Search for “Hot Player” or “IBO Player” and install it.

2

Note your device ID

Open the app. It shows a MAC address or Device ID (and a Device Key for IBO Player). Keep that screen open.

3

Add your playlist

On your phone or PC, open the app’s official website, enter the ID from your TV and paste the M3U link we sent you on WhatsApp.

4

Restart the app

Close and reopen the app on your TV. Your channels load automatically.

Quick Answers

Setup FAQs

The questions we get asked most while helping customers set up their apps.

Which app is best for my device?+
For Android phones, tablets and TVs we suggest XCIPTV Player. On Samsung and LG TVs use IBO Player or Hot Player. On iPhone and iPad use Smarters Player Lite. Not sure? Message us and we will pick one for you.
Why do the Samsung and LG apps ask for payment?+
Free IPTV players are generally not available in the Samsung and LG app stores. The players that work well there, like IBO Player and Hot Player, give a free trial and then ask for a paid activation. That fee goes to the app developer, not to us, so only pay on the app’s official website.
Where do I get the Downloader code for Firestick?+
Downloader codes can change when app developers update their links, so we confirm the current code for you on WhatsApp before you install. Tap any “Get Downloader code” button and we will reply with it.
What is the difference between Xtream Codes and M3U?+
Xtream Codes is a server URL, username and password. It loads channels, movies, series and the TV guide in one login. M3U is a single playlist link. Use whichever your app supports. We provide both.
Do these apps come with channels?+
No. These apps are players only. Your channels, movies and series come from your Arsal Digital subscription.
My app says login failed. What should I do?+
Check that the server URL, username and password are typed exactly as we sent them, with no extra spaces. If it still fails, send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we will fix it quickly.
